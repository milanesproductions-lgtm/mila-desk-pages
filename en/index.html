The difference

Fewer tabs, fewer forgotten brands.

With Mila.Desk

  • You see the brands waiting for your reply in one panel.

Frequently asked questions

What people usually ask us.

Does Mila.Desk ask for my Amazon password?

No, never. The extension reads the pages you already see with your own session. Your Mila.Desk account is separate and has its own password.

Is it affiliated with Amazon?

No. Mila.Desk is an independent tool, not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Amazon.

Does it send messages to brands without me knowing?

No. A message is only sent when you press the button and confirm it. In Message brands, each brand can also be written to only once a week.

Where is my data stored?

Your conversations (brand, number of messages and dates, never the text), your collaborations and your account are stored in the Mila.Desk cloud, separated by user. Your quick messages, your templates, which brands you wrote to and your sales have a private copy in your account that only you can see. Video Intelligence commissions stay in your browser.

How do I activate Premium?

Install the extension, create your account and confirm your email. In Settings → Your access, choose a plan, with 14 days free the first time, or enter an access code if you were given one.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, anytime, from Settings in the extension.
