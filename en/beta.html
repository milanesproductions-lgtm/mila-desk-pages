Install the Mila.Desk beta

Thanks for trying Mila.Desk, the assistant for Amazon Creator Connections creators. Installing it takes about 3 minutes in Google Chrome (Mac or Windows).

Install it from Mila.Desk on the Chrome Web Store.

1. Install the extension

  1. In Chrome, open Mila.Desk on the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Click Add to Chrome, then Add extension.
  3. Pin the icon: click the puzzle piece 🧩 next to the address bar, then the pin 📌 next to Mila.Desk.

2. Create your account and activate Premium

  1. Click the orange Mila.Desk icon, then ⚙ (Settings). If it shows up in Spanish, choose English under 🌐 Idioma · Language.
  2. Click No account? Sign up. Enter your Amazon creator username, your email and a new password (don't use your Amazon one), check the box and sign up.
  3. Open the confirmation email and confirm your account. Then go back to Mila.Desk and sign in.
  4. Send your account email to whoever invited you or to milanes@mila-desk.com. Once Premium is activated, close and reopen Mila.Desk. If you got a code, enter it in Settings → ⭐ Your access and click ACTIVATE.

3. Start using it

  1. Go to Creator Connections with your Amazon account, as usual. Mila.Desk never asks for your Amazon password.
  2. In Mila.Desk, click FULL ANALYSIS the first time. After that, ↻ UPDATE CONVERSATIONS is enough.
  3. In Settings → 💬 Saved messages, save your intro message and your address. They'll show up as buttons inside the Messages chat.
  4. Browse the campaigns: you'll see the Radar (spots, closing date and budget). On Amazon product pages you'll see Video Intelligence.

Updates

Chrome installs new versions by itself. Your account and your data are kept. If a button doesn't show up on Amazon, reload the page.

Something wrong, or got ideas?

Use Settings → 💬 Suggestions and bugs in Mila.Desk, or email us at milanes@mila-desk.com with a screenshot. Your feedback helps us improve it.
