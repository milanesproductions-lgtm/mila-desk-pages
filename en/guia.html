User guide

Your first day with Mila.Desk, step by step.

What to install, what to tap first, what each result means and what to do about it. Follow the steps in order; each one builds on the last.

Google Chrome on desktop Never asks for your Amazon password Each feature says whether it's Free or Premium The brands and products in the screenshots are examples
Before you start

Where do I start?

I just installed it and already have brand chats

Start with Full analysis. It reads all your conversations and builds your panel. Follow the guide from step 1.

I already ran my first analysis

Use Update conversations. It only checks what's new, so it's faster. Go to your daily routine.

I'm new to Creator Connections and have no chats

Start with the Radar and Free Samples. The analysis won't have anything to show yet. Create your account and jump to step 7.

1

Install the extension

  1. In Google Chrome on your computer, open Mila.Desk on the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Click Add to Chrome, then Add extension.
  3. Click the puzzle piece 🧩 next to the address bar, then the pin 📌 next to Mila.Desk. That keeps the icon always in view.
2

Create your account and activate your access

  1. Click the Mila.Desk icon, then ⚙ and No account? Sign up. If it shows up in Spanish, choose English under 🌐 Idioma · Language.
  2. Enter your Creator Connections username, your email and a new password. Don't use your Amazon one.
  3. Open the confirmation email, confirm your account and go back to Mila.Desk to sign in. If it doesn't arrive, check your spam folder.
  4. To activate Premium, go to the ⭐ Your access section and choose the monthly or yearly plan: the first time you get 14 days free. If you got a code, enter it there and click ACTIVATE.

You can start without Premium too. Everything marked Free works the same. Premium features show a lock 🔒.

3Free

Run your first analysis

This is the first thing to do if you already have conversations. Mila.Desk reads them with your Amazon session and tells you where each brand stands.

Full analysis

Checks all your conversations again, one by one.

Use it the first time, or if a number doesn't add up.

↻ Update conversations

Checks only new conversations or ones with new messages.

Use it every other day.

  1. In Mila.Desk, on the ① ANALYZE tab, click ↗ GO TO CREATOR CONNECTIONS and sign in to Amazon if asked.
  2. With that Amazon tab in front, open Mila.Desk again and click FULL ANALYSIS.
  3. Watch the progress bar. How long it takes depends on how many conversations you have. You can close the Mila.Desk window and reopen it to check how it's going.
  4. When it's done, click SEE RESULTS →.

Important: the analysis only starts if the tab in front is an Amazon tab. While it runs, don't close or reload that tab.

4Free

Understand your results

On the ② RESULTS tab you'll see your brands with a label. Always start with the To answer filter: those brands are waiting for you.

LabelWhat it meansWhat to do
To answerThe brand sent the last message.Answer these first.
I repliedThe brand has replied before, and the last message is yours.Wait for their reply.
No replyThe brand has never answered.Decide whether it's worth following up or moving on.
RepliedThis filter gathers every brand that has ever answered you.Use it to see your active contacts.

How to read a row. Under the name you'll see something like “2 conversations · 8 messages · 3 from the brand”. A brand can have several conversations, and Mila.Desk groups them into one row.

Each row's buttons. ＋ COLLAB. adds the brand to your collaborations. OPEN takes you straight to the chat (Premium). The search box finds a brand by name.

If “Need review” shows up in the summary, Mila.Desk couldn't classify that conversation. Check it yourself on Amazon. It never stores the text of your messages, only the brand, the counts and the dates.

5Free list · Premium tracking

Organize your collaborations

When a brand is already working with you, add it with ＋ COLLAB. You'll then find it on the ③ COLLABORATIONS tab.

  1. Tap the 🔴 Need a reply filter to see which of your collaborations are waiting for you.
  2. With Premium, set each brand's stage: Pending, Product on its way, Product received, Content in progress, Content delivered or Finished.
  3. With Premium, your accepted campaigns link to each brand, one per product. No more pasting links by hand.
6Premium

Set up your quick messages

Save your intro and your address once, and use them with one button inside the Amazon chat.

  1. Go to ⚙, under 💬 Saved messages, and click ＋ ADD MESSAGE.
  2. Write the text. Put {brand} wherever you want the chat's brand name to appear.
  3. Choose how it works. With ✎ the text is typed in the box and you press Send. With ➤ it's sent right away. Your address can go out one line per message, so Amazon doesn't squash the lines together.
  4. Open a chat in Creator Connections Messages. Your buttons show up above the text box.

Tip: keep “Ask for confirmation before sending” turned on. Before sending, Mila.Desk asks you. If Amazon warns about personal information, as with an address, and you confirm, it presses Continue for you. These messages are saved only in your browser.

7Premium

Pick campaigns with the Radar

You don't have to tap anything. Go to the Creator Connections campaigns and you'll see the Radar on each card and inside the campaign detail.

SignalWhen it showsWhat to do
Spots openNone of the signals below.Take your time.
Filling up50% of spots taken, 7 days or less to close, or 30% of budget or less.Decide soon if you're interested.
Last chance80% of spots taken, 3 days or less to close, or 10% of budget or less.If it suits you, accept it today.
ClosedNo spots left, no budget left, or the campaign ended.Look for another one.

What to look at in the detail: how many spots are left, why it's flagged, the closing date, the remaining budget, the commission and whether the brand sends samples. Some campaigns don't show their spots; in that case, go by the date and the budget.

⭐ Follow campaignThe campaign shows up in ⑤ TO-DO with its spots left, so you accept it in time.
📌 Follow up with this brandThe brand goes to the Follow up list in Message brands, so you can write again if it doesn't reply.
8Premium

Write to many brands with Message brands

Open Mila.Desk and click 📨 MESSAGE BRANDS. A page opens with four steps.

  1. Choose the brands. Pick a category (or type a brand name) and click FIND BRANDS. You get 100 brands with open campaigns, sorted by commission. Load 100 more brands shows more.
  2. Write your message. Each block arrives as a separate message. With the Brand, Campaign, Product link and Commission buttons, each brand gets its own details.
  3. Review and send. Check the preview and click SEND. It goes to one brand at a time, with a pause, and you can stop it anytime.
🟢 NewYou've never talked to that brand. Only these are ticked by default.
🟡 You wroteYou already wrote from this page or in your chats. It says when you can write again.
🔵 Already replied / In collaborationsYou already talk to that brand: write from its chat.

📌 Follow up with brands that don't reply

Click 📌 on a brand (or 📌 Follow up with this brand in the Radar) and it goes to step 4 of the page. ↻ CHECK REPLIES checks on Amazon whether they answered. You can tick the ones that are ready and send them your short message with ✉ FOLLOW UP.

Once a week: each brand can be written to only once a week from Mila.Desk, so Amazon doesn't see it as repeated messages. Nothing is sent without your click. By sending, you accept Creator Connections' communication rules.

9Premium

Accept campaigns and send your videos

Open Mila.Desk and click 🎬 MY VIDEOS' CAMPAIGNS. Then click FIND CAMPAIGNS: Mila.Desk checks the products in your videos one by one, with a pause.

  1. Open campaigns: tick the ones you want and click Accept. Only the ones you tick are accepted.
  2. Accepted campaigns with no video sent: the campaigns you already accepted that have no content, when you have a video of the same product or a variant. Each one shows the video and the link that will be sent.
  3. Tick the ones you want and click 📤 SEND VIDEOS. If you tick Let the brand know, each brand gets your message after the video is sent.

Campaigns you already sent a video to show “✅ Video sent” and are never sent again. In the popup's ⑤ TO-DO you also see your accepted campaigns still missing content and the days they have left.

10Premium

Check your videos with Video Intelligence

It shows up on its own too. Go to Manage videos, your Storefront or a product page on Amazon, and look at the Mila.Desk panel.

Video positionWhere your video shows up in the product's gallery or carousel.
Estimated commissionWhat that product could earn you. It's an estimate, not a confirmed payment.
Monthly purchasesHow much the product is being bought, according to what Amazon shows.
PriceThe current price and the prices your browser has seen in the last 30 days.
Estimated monthly revenueA rough calculation from the product's price and monthly purchases.
Campaigns“Active · delivering” or “Joined · not delivering yet”. “Possible brand campaign” means it still needs confirming, so check it with See campaign ↗.

How to use it: before recording, open the product and compare purchases, commission and campaigns. If a panel says “Checking…”, wait a few seconds. The first time takes a little longer.

11Premium

Find brands with Free Samples

It's a shared list of brands that send free samples. Each brand is approved after its proof is reviewed.

  1. Open the ④ FREE SAMPLES tab and search by name, or filter by category.
  2. Click ↗ SEE CAMPAIGNS to open that brand's campaigns in Creator Connections.
  3. Did a brand send you a sample? Click ＋ ADD BRAND and paste a screenshot that proves it. You can also report it from the Radar. It'll show up in the list once approved.

These brands' campaigns show up in the Radar with “Sample ✓”.

12Premium

Find out what sold

When there are only a few sales, Amazon doesn't tell you which product sold. Mila.Desk works it out from each day's totals and the price and commission of the products it already knows.

  1. Open your reports in Amazon Associates, on the Commissions tab, and let the table by date show up.
  2. Click the 📊 What sold? · Mila.Desk button on that page. It opens in a new tab.
  3. Read each day's label using the table below.
🟢 CertainOnly one of the products Mila.Desk knows fits.
🟡 LikelySeveral combinations are possible, or only part of the data matches.

Days when Mila.Desk can't identify the product don't show up in the list.

Keep in mind: it's an estimate, not Amazon data. A sale of a product Mila.Desk doesn't know can't be detected. The more products you view with Video Intelligence, the more it has to compare against. This data has a private copy in your account.

13

Your Mila.Desk routine

Every day

  • Open Amazon and click ↻ UPDATE CONVERSATIONS.
  • Answer the To answer filter.
  • Look at campaigns flagged Last chance.
  • Check ⑤ TO-DO: campaigns missing content and campaigns you follow.

Every week

  • Update your collaborations' stages.
  • Look for new brands in Free Samples and in Message brands.
  • Click ↻ CHECK REPLIES in 📌 Follow up.
  • Send your new videos to your accepted campaigns.
  • Check What sold? and your videos.
  • Save a copy with ↓ EXPORT BACKUP.

If something goes wrong

It says “The active tab isn't Amazon”

Bring an Amazon tab to the front, open Mila.Desk and tap the analysis button again.

It says “An analysis is already running”

Wait for it to finish. If you closed the Amazon tab while it was running, try again after about 5 minutes.

The analysis stops or shows a session error

Check that you're still signed in to Amazon and to Mila.Desk, reload the Amazon page and try again.

I don't see the Radar, the video panel or my message buttons

Reload the Amazon page. Also check that your Premium access is active in Settings, under “⭐ Your access”.

I forgot my Mila.Desk password

On the sign-in screen, tap “Forgot your password?” and enter your email. It's a separate password from your Amazon one.

Does Mila.Desk send messages without me knowing?

No. A message is only typed or sent when you press its button. In Message brands, each brand is also written to at most once a week.

Amazon held a message

Amazon sometimes holds messages with personal details, like an address. Mila.Desk lets you know and stops if it happens several times in a row. Remove those details from the message and try again another day.

I changed computers: do I lose my messages?

No. Your quick messages, your templates, the 📌 list and which brands you wrote to have a copy in your account. Sign in and open Mila.Desk: they come back by themselves.

I want to change the colors or delete my account

Both are in Settings (⚙). If you delete your account, export a backup first: it can't be undone.
