In effect since November 3, 2026 (updated November 13, 2026) · “Mila.Desk – Assistant for Amazon Creators” extension

Mila.Desk is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Amazon. Amazon, Amazon Associates and Creator Connections are trademarks of Amazon.com, Inc. or its affiliates.

1. What Mila.Desk is for

Mila.Desk helps Amazon creators manage Creator Connections: the status of their conversations with brands, their collaborations, the available campaigns and information about their videos. We only collect the data strictly needed for that purpose.

3. Who can see your data

  • You. Your conversations, collaborations and history are separated by account: no other user can see them.
  • Mila.Desk administrators see Free Samples requests, including the proof image and the username of whoever sent it, only to review them; the account list (username, email, sign-up date, whether you confirmed your email and access type) to give and remove access; and the suggestions and reports you send, with your username and email, to review them and reply to you. Administrators don't see your conversations or your collaborations.
  • Other users only see the names of approved Free Samples brands and public product data.
  • Providers: the data is hosted on Supabase, which acts as a data processor. Account emails (confirmation and password recovery) are sent through Resend, which receives your address to deliver them. The mila-desk.com pages are hosted on GitHub Pages and load their fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive technical data about your visit, such as your IP address. If you buy Premium, payment is processed by our payment provider, which acts as the seller and handles taxes and invoices. We don't sell or rent your data, we don't share it with anyone else, and we don't use it for advertising or for purposes unrelated to the extension.

4. Security

All communication uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). The database enforces per-user access rules (Row Level Security), and proof images are stored in private storage.

5. How long it is kept

Your account data is kept as long as the account exists. If you delete your account, your data and your proof images are deleted, except the free trial fingerprint, which is kept so the trial isn't repeated; Free Samples brands that were already approved are kept for the community, but without your name or any of your data. Data that only lives in your browser is deleted when you uninstall the extension, and Video Intelligence's temporary caches clear themselves over time.

6. Your rights

You can delete your account and its data at any time from the extension's settings (“Delete my account”), and export a copy from the same screen. You can also ask for a copy, a correction or a deletion by email. For any request, email us at milanes@mila-desk.com.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change what data we collect or how we use it, we will update this page and let you know inside the extension before the change affects you.
